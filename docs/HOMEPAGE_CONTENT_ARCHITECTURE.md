# WorkSync Homepage Content Architecture

## Scope and editorial rules

This document defines homepage content only. It does not create a route, JSX, page assembly, CMS integration, or media assets.

The homepage narrative moves from **who WorkSync is**, to **what it does**, **why buyers can trust the delivery model**, **how engagements work**, **where the platform is going**, and **how to begin a conversation**.

- Use short, concrete sentences. Address founders, HR leaders, and business owners directly.
- Do not claim market leadership, guaranteed outcomes, unsupported scale, or fictional customer results.
- Publish trust indicators, statistics, certifications, customer logos, and testimonials only after legal and client approval.
- Use real India-based workplace, operations, and decision-making imagery. Avoid staged handshakes, call-centre clichés, and generic “people around laptop” stock images.
- Treat all CTA labels as proposed navigation labels. Confirm their destination before implementation.

## Global SEO and accessibility direction

- **Proposed title:** `WorkSync | Recruitment, Staffing & HR Services Across India`
- **Proposed meta description:** `WorkSync supports recruitment, staffing and HR operations for businesses across India with technology-enabled, human-first workforce solutions.`
- Use one `h1` in Hero. All major section titles are `h2`; cards use subordinate heading levels.
- Keep “recruitment”, “staffing”, “HR as a Service”, “workforce solutions”, and “India” natural and contextual. Do not repeat exact-match phrases mechanically.
- Every content-managed image requires `alt`, `decorative`, and `focalPoint` fields. Decorative media must be excluded from the accessible name.
- All motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no content may be hidden behind animation.
